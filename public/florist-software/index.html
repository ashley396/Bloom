CHOOSING FLORIST SOFTWARE

What to actually look for in florist software

"Florist software" covers a lot of ground, POS, websites, marketing, AI. Here's a practical way to evaluate it, and where Florisyn stands against each item.

Buyer's Guide

A practical evaluation checklist

  • Floral-specific POS and recipe costing, not generic retail
  • Real inventory built around stems and recipes, not fixed SKUs
  • Delivery-aware order management
  • A website that's genuinely yours, with your own domain
  • Transparent, published pricing with no long-term contract
  • AI features, if any, that stay editable and require your approval

How Florisyn fits, item by item

POS & recipe costing

Built around custom arrangements and recipe-based inventory, not fixed SKUs.

Delivery-aware orders

Delivery or pickup captured at intake, with routing on Pro and Premium plans.

Your own website

Website Studio with a custom domain option, Pro and Premium plans.

Transparent pricing

Three published plans, 14-day free trial, no long-term contract — see pricing.

Editable AI

Lily, Rose, and the AI Suite draft and suggest; you approve before anything goes out.

What's not built yet

In the interest of not overselling: Florisyn does not currently offer a public marketplace of independent reviews, nor a dedicated event-planning suite beyond order and pricing management for weddings. If a specific capability matters to your shop, it's worth confirming directly rather than assuming.

Yes, all three plans and their prices are published on the pricing page.
