ORDER MANAGEMENT

Every order, tracked from the phone call to the doorstep

Orders taken across the phone, the counter, and online can slip through the cracks when they're not tracked in one place. Here's how Florisyn keeps each one visible until it's actually delivered.

An order's real lifecycle

  1. Intake

    An order is captured with the details that matter: recipient, occasion, delivery or pickup, and any card message.

  2. Design & recipe

    The order connects to the recipe your designer actually builds it from.

  3. Production status

    You can see what stage an order is in without walking the shop floor to ask.

  4. Delivery or pickup

    Delivery orders can be routed and dispatched, Pro and Premium plans; pickup orders stay just as visible.

  5. Payment & closeout

    The order closes out with payment recorded against it, not tracked separately.
