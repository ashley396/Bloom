Common questions

Can the AI place orders or send messages on its own?

No, Florisyn's AI features are built to draft and suggest; a person reviews and approves before anything goes out.

What data does the AI use?

It's grounded in your own shop's data, products, customers, and recipes, rather than generic, unrelated content.

Is AI included on the Starter plan?

Lily and Rose are included on Pro and Premium plans; the AI Suite is a Premium feature.
