SEASONAL FLORIST PLANNING

Funeral and sympathy flower work

Sympathy work isn't seasonal the way Valentine's Day is — it happens year-round, usually on short notice, and it calls for a different kind of care than a celebratory order.

SympathyFuneral Flowers

By the Florisyn Team · Published August 20, 2026

What's different about sympathy orders

  • Turnaround is almost always short — often needed within a day or two of the order, not weeks out
  • Orders frequently involve coordinating directly with a funeral home on delivery timing and setup, not just the customer
  • Piece types are more standardized than everyday arrangements (standing sprays, casket sprays, wreaths), which makes recipe-based costing especially useful
  • The tone of every customer interaction is different — this is one of the few order types where a rushed or overly casual conversation genuinely lands wrong

Operational basics

  1. Taking the order

    Confirm the funeral home name, service date and time, and delivery deadline up front — sympathy deliveries usually have a hard time window tied to the service, not a flexible delivery day.

  2. Building the piece

    Standard piece types (sprays, wreaths, baskets) benefit from having a real recipe on file, so pricing and stem usage stay consistent even when the order comes in with little lead time.

  3. Delivering

    Confirm the funeral home's own delivery and setup expectations — some prefer flowers delivered and placed before a specific time, others coordinate directly with on-site staff.

  4. Following up

    A short, genuine follow-up (not a sales message) is appropriate after a sympathy order in a way it wouldn't be after a birthday bouquet.

Common questions

How much lead time do sympathy orders usually need?

Often very little — same-day or next-day is common, since services are typically scheduled only a few days after a death. Keeping a flexible production schedule helps absorb this.

Do I need a relationship with local funeral homes?

Many florists who do regular sympathy work build direct relationships with local funeral homes for delivery coordination, though it's not strictly required to take sympathy orders.

Are sympathy pieces priced differently from everyday arrangements?

The piece types (standing sprays, casket sprays, wreaths) are more standardized, which makes it easier to build accurate recipes for them — the same cost-plus-labor pricing approach used for everyday arrangements still applies.
