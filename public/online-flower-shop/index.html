The problem with a bolted-on online store

When online orders live in a separate platform from your POS and inventory, someone has to manually keep stock and pricing in sync, or you risk selling something online that's already sold out in the shop.

What matters for selling online

  • One product catalog, not two versions to keep in sync
  • Inventory that reflects both in-store and online sales in real time
  • Payments connected to the same order and reporting system
  • A storefront that actually represents your shop, not a generic template
