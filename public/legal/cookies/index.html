How Florisyn uses browser storage and cookies.

Cookie Notice

Florisyn uses limited browser storage and cookies to keep users signed in, protect accounts, remember preferences, and support payment security.

Essential technologies

Florisyn may use session tokens, local browser storage, and essential cookies so the application can authenticate users and remember settings.

Payments and fraud prevention

When Stripe services are used, Stripe may collect device and interaction signals for payment security and fraud prevention under Stripe’s own privacy terms.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser. Disabling essential storage may prevent login, saved preferences, or payment features from working correctly.
