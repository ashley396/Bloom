Accessibility

Our goal is to make public pages and florist software usable by people with a wide range of abilities and assistive technologies.

Our approach

Florisyn aims to use readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, visible labels, responsive layouts, and meaningful alternative text where practical.

Need assistance?

Contact customer service and describe the page, feature, or information you need in an alternative format.
