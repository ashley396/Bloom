CHOOSING A POS FOR YOUR FLOWER SHOP

What a flower shop actually needs from a POS system

Before you switch point-of-sale systems, it helps to know what to actually evaluate. Here's what matters for a flower shop specifically, and how Florisyn's POS holds up against it.

Buyer's GuidePOS Evaluation

What to evaluate in a flower shop POS

  • Handles custom, made-to-order arrangements, not just fixed-price SKUs
  • Ties a sale to a recipe and real inventory, not a generic stock count
  • Captures delivery details at checkout, not as an afterthought
  • Keeps customer history you can actually use for repeat business
  • Transparent, published pricing with no long-term contract
  • No per-order wire-service-style fee for orders you take yourself

How Florisyn's POS measures up

Custom arrangements

Products are built around recipes, not fixed SKUs, so custom orders are the default, not a workaround.

Recipe-aware inventory

Sales draw from real inventory tied to the recipe behind each product.

Delivery-aware checkout

Delivery or pickup is part of the order from the start.

Customer history

Every order ties back to a real customer record.

Transparent pricing

Plans and prices are published — see current pricing.

Switching without losing what you have

Moving point-of-sale systems is a real decision, not just a feature checklist. If you're mid-evaluation, it's worth asking any vendor, Florisyn included, exactly what happens to your existing customer list and order history during a switch before you commit.

Common questions

Do I have to switch everything at once?

Most shops move gradually, starting with new orders in the new system while wrapping up existing work in the old one. Talk to Florisyn support about a plan that fits your shop.

What happens to my existing customer list?

That depends on where your data currently lives; contact Florisyn support to talk through your specific situation before switching.

Is there a wire-service-style fee on orders I take myself?

No. Florisyn does not charge a per-order wire fee on orders you take and fulfill yourself.
