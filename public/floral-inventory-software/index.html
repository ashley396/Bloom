FLORAL INVENTORY

Know what's in the cooler, and what each arrangement really costs

Floral inventory isn't like retail inventory. It's perishable, sold by the stem or bunch, and every arrangement is really a recipe of smaller ingredients. Generic inventory tools don't model that.

InventoryRecipe Costing

The problem with generic inventory tools

Most inventory software tracks a single SKU going in and out. Floral inventory needs to track individual stems and hard goods that get combined into arrangements, and it needs to do that while stock is perishable and prices change with the season.

What matters in floral inventory

  • Tracks stock by stem or bunch, not just a generic unit count
  • Low-stock alerts before you're mid-order and out of a key flower
  • Recipes tie ingredient cost to what you actually charge
  • Visibility into what's selling versus what's sitting and going to waste

Common questions

What's a 'recipe' in Florisyn?

A recipe is the worksheet behind a product: every flower, greenery, and supply it actually takes to make, tied to the price you charge.

Does this prevent overselling a flower I'm out of?

Low-stock alerts are designed to flag shortages before they become a problem at checkout.

Can I track waste or shrink?

Recipe-based inventory gives you the data to see the gap between what you bought and what you sold; Florisyn does not currently have a dedicated waste-logging workflow beyond that.
