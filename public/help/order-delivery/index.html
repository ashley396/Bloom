How Florisyn supports florist fulfillment.

Order & Delivery Info

Florisyn helps florists manage customer orders, pickups, deliveries, payment status, addresses, notes, and production progress.

Florist customer orders

Florisyn software customers use the order center to record the recipient, delivery address, card message, requested time, flower recipe, payment status, and fulfillment progress.

Florisyn software delivery

Florisyn is an online software service. Access begins after account setup and any required email confirmation. No physical software shipment is required.

Flower delivery responsibility

Individual florist subscribers remain responsible for their own flower orders, delivery areas, delivery policies, substitutions, refunds, and customer service.
