FOUNDING FLORISTS

Florisyn — made from the florist's side of the counter.

Founding Florists is a collaborative early-access program for independent flower shops. You get a real, full 14-day free trial; we get honest feedback from florists actually running a shop day to day.

Early Access14-Day Free Trial

Why this program exists

Florisyn started from real problems on the florist's side of the counter — the same order taken twice, a recipe cost nobody actually tracked, a wire-service fee eating into a margin that was already thin. Software built without that context tends to solve the wrong problems well. Founding Florists is how we keep solving the right ones: by building alongside the florists actually using it, not guessing on their behalf.

What being a Founding Florist means

A real trial, not a demo

The same 14-day free trial every Florisyn plan includes — full access to POS, orders, inventory, and the rest of the platform in your own shop, with your own data.

Feedback that actually reaches us

What's confusing, what's missing, what genuinely helps — sent directly to the team building Florisyn, not routed through a support queue.

A say in what gets built next

Founding Florists' feedback directly shapes what Florisyn prioritizes. You won't get a vote on every feature, but you will be heard.

No pressure, no obligation

Feedback is invited, never required. Your trial works the same either way, and nothing you say is published without asking you first.

What we're asking for

Just honesty. If something in Florisyn doesn't work the way a real shop needs it to, tell us — even if that's not what we hoped to hear. That's the entire ask.

What this isn't

To be straightforward about scope: this isn't a numbered, limited program with a fixed number of spots, and it isn't a special 30-day trial — every Florisyn trial, Founding Florist or not, is the same real 14-day free trial with no credit card required. We'd rather describe the program accurately than promise something we'd have to walk back later.
