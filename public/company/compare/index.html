Where Florisyn doesn't win

To be direct about trade-offs: Florisyn is a web app, not a native iOS/Android app, and Florist Network is a newer, smaller order network than the decades-established wire services — if your shop relies heavily on incoming out-of-town wire orders, that established reach is a real factor to weigh. Hana Florist POS also publishes a no-monthly-fee, pay-per-order option that may suit a very small or seasonal shop better than a flat monthly plan.
