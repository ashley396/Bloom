Pricing questions

Is there really a free trial?

Yes. Starter, Pro, and Premium each include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to create your account.

What happens after the trial?

You choose monthly or annual billing for the plan you selected. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your subscription center.

How does annual billing work?

Pay once per year at the price shown (for example, $590/yr for Starter). You get two months free compared to paying monthly — the note under each plan shows your savings and the monthly equivalent.

Are payment processing fees included?

Florisyn subscription pricing covers the software. Payment processors may charge their own disclosed processing fees on customer transactions.
