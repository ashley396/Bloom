About Florisyn

Where Your Passion Flowers. Florisyn is The Operating System for Modern Florists — a simpler, more affordable way to manage orders, customers, inventory, recipes, deliveries, payments, marketing, and growth without depending on expensive wire-service systems.

Our mission

Our mission is to help independent flower shops protect their profits, serve customers beautifully, and compete with confidence. Florisyn brings everyday florist work into one connected platform while keeping the florist in control.
