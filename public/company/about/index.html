About BloomTech

BloomTech was created to give independent florists a simpler, more affordable way to manage orders, customers, inventory, recipes, deliveries, payments, marketing, and growth without depending on expensive wire-service systems.

Our mission

Our mission is to help independent flower shops protect their profits, serve customers beautifully, and compete with confidence. BloomTech brings everyday florist work into one connected platform while keeping the florist in control.
