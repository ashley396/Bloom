Software that follows your shop's actual day, start to close

Flower shop software should match how a shop actually runs, not force the day into a generic retail template. Here's what that looks like from open to close.

Daily WorkflowAll-in-One

A flower shop's day, and where Florisyn fits

  1. Orders come in

    Walk-in, phone, and online orders land in one order center, with delivery or pickup captured up front.

  2. Design & recipe

    Each product can carry a recipe — the stems, greenery, and supplies it actually takes — so design work stays connected to cost.

  3. Inventory updates

    A sale draws down real inventory, with low-stock alerts before you run out mid-week.

  4. Delivery gets organized

    Delivery orders can be routed and dispatched to drivers, Pro and Premium plans.

  5. Payment & closeout

    Payments, invoices, and the day's numbers land in the same dashboard you started the morning with.

  6. The website stays current

    Website Studio keeps your public site and online ordering in sync with the same product catalog, Pro and Premium plans.

Common questions

Does this replace my existing cash register?

Florisyn's POS and order center is designed to be the system you check out from — most shops that adopt it use it as their primary point of sale.

Do I need separate tools for inventory, a website, and marketing?

No, those are built into Florisyn's Pro and Premium plans rather than being separate subscriptions.

What's the difference between plans?

Starter covers core POS, orders, and inventory. Pro adds delivery routing, the website builder, and AI assistants. Premium adds multi-location and wholesale marketplace tools. See full plan details.
