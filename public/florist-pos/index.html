FLORIST POS

A point-of-sale system built around how flower shops actually sell

Most point-of-sale software is built for fixed-SKU retail. A flower shop sale is rarely that simple: a custom arrangement, a delivery address, a card message, and ingredients that need to come out of inventory correctly.

Point of SaleCheckoutOrder Center

The problem with generic retail POS

Generic POS systems assume every item is a fixed SKU at a fixed price. Flower shops sell custom arrangements, walk-in bouquets, and delivery jobs, often several kinds in the same transaction, and a register that can't represent that pushes real work into a separate notebook or spreadsheet.

What actually matters in a florist POS

  • Rings up custom arrangements, not just fixed-price items
  • Connects each sale to the ingredients it used, not just a revenue total
  • Distinguishes delivery orders from walk-in and pickup at checkout
  • Keeps a real customer record and order history, not just a receipt
  • Handles split payments and partial deposits cleanly
  • Works comfortably on the shop floor, not just in a back-office browser tab

Common questions

Does Florisyn's POS connect to inventory automatically?

Yes. Products can be built with a recipe, the actual stems, greenery, and hard goods used, so a sale ties back to real inventory, not just a revenue line.

Can I take orders for both delivery and pickup?

Yes, delivery and pickup are both captured as part of the order, and delivery orders can be organized with Florisyn's delivery routing tools on Pro and Premium plans.

Can a customer split a payment?

Yes, Florisyn's payment center supports handling a balance across more than one payment.

Do I need special hardware?

Florisyn runs in a browser on the devices your shop already has; specific hardware needs, like a receipt printer, depend on your existing setup.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial and there's no long-term contract. See current plans and pricing.
