# Florisyn — Wholesaler Manual

*For shops that sell wholesale to other florists through the Florisyn Marketplace. Your seller dashboard lives inside the same Florisyn app you already use as a florist — it isn't a separate login.*

---

## Getting started

You don't need a separate account to sell wholesale — sign in the same way you always do. Open **Marketplace** in your sidebar, switch to the **Sell** tab, and click **Open seller dashboard**. From there, everything in this manual is one click away.

If you haven't sold wholesale before, you'll need to connect **Stripe Connect** first (so buyers can pay you directly and Florisyn never touches your money) and complete **florist verification** if you plan to buy wholesale from other sellers too — see the Verification section below.

---

## Your seller dashboard

*Products, inventory, orders, customers, shipping, and pricing in one workspace.*

This is your home base as a wholesale seller — everything below is a panel inside it.

## Products

Add, publish, or archive wholesale listings. A listing only becomes visible to buyers once you publish it — save your work as a draft and come back to it anytime before then. **Archive** takes a listing down without deleting your history for it.

## Bulk CSV import

Have a lot of products to list at once? Import them from a spreadsheet instead of adding them one at a time — the import validates your file first, so you can fix problems before anything goes live.

## Best sellers

A quick view of which of your listings are moving, so you know what to keep stocked.

## Low-stock alerts

Get flagged automatically when a listing's inventory is running low, so you're not caught out by a sudden buyer order.

## Customer pricing tiers

Offer different prices to different tiers of buyers — a volume discount for your best customers, standard pricing for everyone else.

## Shipping profiles

Set up how you ship — rates, zones, and defaults — once, so you don't have to re-enter shipping details on every order.

## Orders

Every wholesale order that's come in, with its own status you update as you fulfill it — the buyer sees this status too, so keep it current.

## Verification

Before you can *buy* wholesale from other Florisyn sellers, you'll need to submit business verification: your legal business name, owner, contact details, business address, and tax ID. An admin reviews and approves it — until it's approved, checkout is blocked with a clear message telling you exactly what's missing, never a silent failure. Once approved, your verification stays valid for a set period and renews the same way.

*(Selling wholesale and buying wholesale are separate permissions — you can list products for sale without needing buyer verification, but you'll need it the moment you want to purchase from someone else's storefront.)*

---

## Getting paid

Wholesale payments run through **Stripe Connect** — you'll be prompted to connect (or finish connecting) your Stripe account the first time it's needed. Once connected, buyer payments go straight to you; Florisyn's platform fee is deducted automatically at checkout, and you never have to invoice separately or chase payment.

---

## Getting help

Use the **Support** button in your sidebar — the same one every florist uses — to open this manual or reach Florisyn support directly.
