BLOOM v21.1 — ACCESS, LOGIN, FOOTER & PAYMENT METHODS

1. Upload/deploy the complete Bloom-main folder to Netlify.
2. In Netlify, confirm Publish directory is: public
3. Confirm Functions directory is: netlify/functions
4. Keep your existing Supabase and Stripe environment variables.
5. Open your admin page at either:
   https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/admin
   https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/admin.html
6. Open customer login at:
   https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/login
7. Open customer signup at:
   https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/signup
8. In Stripe Dashboard > Settings > Payment methods, enable the eligible methods you want (for example Affirm, Afterpay/Clearpay, or Klarna). Stripe decides which options appear based on eligibility, amount, currency, and customer location.
9. Hard refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R. On phone, close and reopen the app.

IMPORTANT: Affirm/Afterpay are for eligible customer order payments. They are not generally available for Bloom's recurring software subscription Checkout.
