# Bloom v9.1 Integrated Workflow

- Automatically creates a delivery stop when a delivery order is saved.
- Carries recipient, address, date, time window, driver, mileage and instructions into Delivery Center.
- Adds delivery counts, active route mileage and a printable driver manifest.
- Saves complete florist design details with every order.
- Replaces unrelated remote stock photos with four local florist-only website illustrations, including hydrangeas.
- Preserves compact receipt printing, settings, payments and existing modules.
