BLOOM v20.2 PROFESSIONAL EDITION — ONE DEPLOYMENT

1. Unzip this package.
2. Upload the complete Bloom-main folder to Netlify exactly as you have done for prior releases.
3. No Supabase migration is required.
4. No new Netlify environment variables are required.
5. After Netlify finishes, open Bloom and press Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows.
6. On a phone, close the installed Bloom app/browser tab completely and reopen it.
7. If Bloom is installed to the home screen, remove and reinstall the home-screen shortcut to force the new app icon to appear.

This release changes appearance only. It does not replace or remove Bloom functions.
