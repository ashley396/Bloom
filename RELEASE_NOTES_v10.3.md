# Bloom X v10.3 — Hey Lily / Hey Rose

- Added opt-in hands-free wake phrases: “Hey Lily” and “Hey Rose.”
- Lily responds warmly: “Yes! What can I do for you?”
- Rose responds with her playful personality: “Yes, honey? What do you need?”
- A command can follow the wake phrase in the same sentence, or be spoken as the next sentence.
- Continuous listening automatically restarts while Bloom remains open.
- Added clear microphone permission, listening, and off-status messages.
- Preserved typed questions and the existing microphone button.

## Browser note
Hands-free speech recognition works best in current Chrome or Edge. The user must click the wake-phrase button once and allow microphone access. The Bloom tab must remain open; browsers do not allow a normal website to listen after it is closed.
